Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

cloud-audit is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive data only for the account that signs in, and only to list, compare, organise and upload that account's own files.
  • Data read from Google APIs stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  • No data about other people is collected. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
